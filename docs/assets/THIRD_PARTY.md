# Third-party assets bundled in this repository

Each folder under `art/sourced/` keeps its own license file; the credit lines below must travel with the assets (CC BY 3.0 and
4.0 ask for the author's name, and the OFL fonts for their license text). Everything here is used unchanged unless the row
says otherwise. The game-icons.net silhouettes are framed into the tiles of `art/icons/` (built with `art/icons.json`);
credit "Icons made by {author}" for them, authors in the game's `art/credits.json`.
