## Content beyond the SRD

The `data/` folders also hold game mechanics from other Wizards of the Coast books (the 2024 Player's Handbook, Dungeon
Master's Guide and Monster Manual, and supplements). That content belongs to Wizards of the Coast; it is **not** licensed by
this repository's MIT license and nothing here grants any right to it. The data files hold mechanics and the project's own
descriptions, not the books' text, and much of it was entered from knowledge of the books and still needs checking against
them (`source.checked_against` on each entry says what was compared). If you reuse this data, check what you are allowed to
publish.
